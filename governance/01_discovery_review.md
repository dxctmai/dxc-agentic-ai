# Review Board 1 — Discovery Review (Session 2)

**Where:** the Tech Lead keeps the team copy in their fork (`teams\team-x\01_discovery.md`); present by screen share.
**Format:** 4 minutes present + 1 minute board questions. One page. Copy this file to `teams\team-x\01_discovery.md` and fill it in.
**Board:** Trainer (Chair) + 2 reviewers from other teams. **Verdict:** Approved · Approved with conditions · Re-present.

---

## 1. Who are the users? (2–3 personas)
| Persona | What they need from IT | What frustrates them today |
|---|---|---|
| *e.g. Sales rep travelling* | *VPN and email working, fast* | *Waits 4 hours for a password reset* |
| | | |

## 2. What hurts most? (top 3 pains — use tickets.csv as evidence)
| Pain | Evidence (count / example ticket) | Cost of the pain |
|---|---|---|
| *Lockouts & password resets* | *~15% of tickets; TKT-0001* | *User blocked; client call missed* |
| | | |

## 3. What will AskIT do — and NOT do?
- **In scope:** *e.g. answer how-to questions from the KB; triage priority*
- **Out of scope (for now):** *e.g. anything for VIP users without a human*

## 4. How will we measure success?
| Metric | Today (estimate) | Target |
|---|---|---|
| *% tickets answered without an agent* | *0%* | *30%* |
| *Time to first answer* | *1 hour* | *< 1 minute* |
| *Wrong answers (hallucination rate)* | — | *< 5%* |

## 5. Risks & open questions for the client
- *e.g. Can AskIT reset passwords by itself, or only guide the user?*
